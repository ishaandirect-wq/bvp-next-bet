        A PERSONAL NOTE

        A note to Bessemer

        You asked applicants to build something rather than just send a CV. Fair enough.

        I’ve spent most of my career building and operating businesses, and recently started experimenting with building my own products using AI. So, for this application, I built NEXT BET.

        The idea is simple: investors have opinions about markets all the time. I wanted a way to challenge those opinions, look at both sides, and keep a record of how my thinking changes as I learn more.

        This is a working experiment, not a finished investment research platform. There’s plenty I’d like to improve, particularly around live data and research accuracy.

        But I wanted to show you how I approach a problem, build something useful around it, and actually get it out into the world.

        — Ishaan Singh
